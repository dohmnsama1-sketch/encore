## Disqualification risks: summary

1. 🚩 **Duplicate-submission rule (#16).** Opener and Encore come from the same entrant. They are differentiated, but the entrant must choose to submit one or state the distinction in both.
2. ⚠️ **Demo must be live and free (#9).** The deploy needs one Vercel re-authorisation by the user. If Vercel stays blocked, use the repo's one-click deploy on any Node host.
3. ⚠️ **Name collision (not a rule, a judging risk).** An unrelated entrant has a public repo `Ritish017/encore-qloo` and a site at `encore-qloo.vercel.app`, *"Encore — a booking agent with taste"*, which also targets independent venues. This is not a rule breach (our first commit, Oct 20, is independent and the concepts differ), but judges could confuse the two. Consider a distinct product name before submitting.
4. ⚠️ **Deadline (#3).** Submit by Oct 30 11:45 pm ET (Oct 31 06:45 Riyadh); target Oct 28.
