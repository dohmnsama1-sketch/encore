    Draw & ROI forecast

    planning estimate

    Each night's draw index (0–100) comes only from Qloo signals: 50% crowd fit (affinity), 25% genre strength, 25% reach (popularity), +5 for audience growth, +5 for a bridge pick. Enter your room's numbers to turn it into guests and money. Expected guests = quiet-night guests + (capacity − quiet-night guests) × draw × conversion. Net = extra guests × (ticket + bar spend) − cost of the night.
