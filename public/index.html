Your crowd already told the world what they love.
Encore turns it into a sold-out month.

Ask a generic AI how to fill a quiet Tuesday and you get "trivia night" — the same answer for every venue on earth. Encore reads the cultural taste of the people who already love your room, tests genres against them, and programmes nights they over-index on. Every pick shows its Qloo score.

      Use Encore from any agent (MCP)

      Encore is also a public, stateless MCP server. Add it to Claude, Cursor or any MCP client and ask: "Plan next month for Smalls Jazz Club and gut-check a salsa night."

      Tools: encore_plan_month, encore_gut_check, encore_crowd_profile. Local stdio: node bin/encore-mcp.js.
